# HR Operations Guide

## Employee List, Search, and Filter

Use Employees to search, filter, and open employee records. Visibility follows role permissions and access scopes.

## Employee 360 Profile

Employee 360 is the main workspace for profile, job, contacts, contracts, documents, leave, attendance, roster, payroll, payment methods, assets, notes, lifecycle, and audit history.

## Creating Employees

Use the Employee Creation Wizard or onboarding workflow. Draft/onboarding employees should be activated only when the readiness validator passes or an authorized warning override is recorded.

## Editing Employee Profile

Admins can update permitted fields. Employee self-service profile changes go through profile update requests and approval where configured.

## User Account Linking

Use Employee 360 or onboarding user access setup to provision or link user accounts. Employee email is preferred when provisioning. Inactive employees must not access self-service.

## Onboarding Cases

Onboarding cases guide setup through employee info, documents, contracts, payroll/payment, user access, attendance/roster, assets/uniforms, and activation readiness. Disabled modules show Not Required.

## Document Uploads

HR official uploads become official documents. Employee self-submissions remain pending until approved. Batch upload supports row-level progress and retry.

## Local/Foreign Document Validation

Local employees are not blocked by Visa or Work Permit by default. Foreign employees can require Passport, Visa, Work Permit, Medical, Insurance, Police Report, Contract, and other configured documents.

## Leave, Attendance, Payroll, and Reports

Review leave requests, approval timelines, attendance corrections, payroll profiles, payment methods, payroll warnings, reports, notifications, and background jobs according to your permissions.

## Disabled Module Behavior

Disabled modules are hidden from operational UI and do not block onboarding/offboarding if marked Not Required by settings.
